# Business Model

## Problem We're Solving

Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) face a painful choice:

1. **Pay for enterprise tools** (Salesforce, Zoho, Microsoft Dynamics) that cost thousands per month and are packed with features they'll never use
2. **Stitch together free tools** — WhatsApp for communication, Excel for inventory, Google Sheets for HR, a random free CRM — and lose hours every week switching between them and reconciling data

There is no affordable, modular, all-in-one platform built specifically for SMBs and startups.

---

## Target Market

### Primary: Small and Medium Businesses (SMBs)
- 10 to 200 employees
- Industries: Retail, Services, Agencies, Clinics, Logistics, Manufacturing
- Currently using a mix of spreadsheets and disconnected tools
- Can't afford or justify enterprise software pricing

### Secondary: Startups
- Early-stage companies (5–50 people) that need to set up operations quickly
- Need flexibility — they don't know yet which tools they'll need long-term
- Value speed of setup over deep customization

### Geography (Initial Focus)
- India (massive SMB market, price-sensitive, high digital adoption)
- Southeast Asia (similar profile)

---

## Market Size

- India has approximately 63 million MSMEs (Micro, Small, Medium Enterprises)
- Global SMB software market is valued at over $500 billion
- Even capturing 0.01% of Indian SMBs at ₹1,000/month = ₹6.3 crore/month revenue

## Pricing Model

### Freemium (Hook)

| Plan | Price | Users | Modules | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0/month | Up to 5 | 1 module | 1 GB |
| Starter | ₹999/month | Up to 15 | 3 modules | 10 GB |
| Pro | ₹2,499/month | Up to 50 | All modules | 50 GB |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | All modules + custom | Unlimited |

### Why Freemium?
- Lowers the barrier to try the product — no credit card required
- Businesses start on Free, grow into paid plans as their team and needs expand
- Word-of-mouth: employees use the tool at work, recommend it to other businesses
- This is exactly how Slack, Notion, and Trello grew

---

## Revenue Streams

1. **Subscription Revenue** — Monthly/annual plans (primary revenue)
2. **Annual Discount** — 2 months free for annual payment (improves cash flow)
3. **Add-ons (future)** — Extra storage, additional modules, white-labeling
4. **Professional Services (future)** — Setup assistance, data migration, custom module development for Enterprise clients

## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Direct Outreach (MVP Launch)
- Reach out to 50 local SMBs directly (retail shops, agencies, clinics)
- Offer free onboarding assistance in exchange for feedback
- Goal: 10 paying customers, 100 free users

### Phase 2: Content Marketing
- Blog posts targeting search terms like "free CRM for small business India", "inventory management software for SMB"
- YouTube tutorials showing how to set up each module
- Goal: Organic traffic driving free signups

### Phase 3: Referral Program
- Existing users get 1 month free for every business they refer that signs up on a paid plan
- Goal: Viral growth within business networks

### Phase 4: Partnerships
- Partner with CA firms, business consultants, and startup incubators who can recommend the platform to their clients
- Goal: B2B2B distribution channel

## Why This Will Work

1. **The problem is real and widespread** — every SMB owner we talk to is frustrated with their current tools
2. **The timing is right** — post-COVID digital adoption among SMBs in India has accelerated dramatically
3. **The model is proven** — Zoho, Freshworks, and Chargebee all built billion-dollar companies solving similar problems for Indian businesses
4. **The technology is accessible** — cloud infrastructure costs have dropped to the point where a small team can build and run a SaaS product affordably

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low adoption — businesses don't switch tools | Freemium removes switching cost. Focus on businesses with no existing tool. |
| Competition from Zoho/Odoo | We're not competing on features — we're competing on simplicity and price for the underserved SMB segment |
| Data security concerns | Clear privacy policy, data encryption, tenant isolation, compliance documentation |
| Technical complexity of multi-tenancy | Well-documented architecture, thorough testing, phased rollout |
| Team bandwidth (final year project) | Scoped MVP with 4 modules. Architecture supports adding more later. |
