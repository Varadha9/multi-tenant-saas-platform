## Research Note

Key insight: Separate tenant email streams and enforce strict authentication to keep deliverability high across SMB customers.  

- Use dedicated subdomains per tenant (e.g., tenant1.mail.yourapp.com) and configure individual SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records; this isolates reputation and simplifies revocation when a tenant is flagged.  
- Implement a tiered sending IP strategy: a warm‑up pool of shared IPs for low‑volume tenants and a premium pool of dedicated IPs for high‑volume tenants, monitoring bounce and complaint rates per IP to trigger automatic re‑allocation.  
- Enforce real‑time validation and suppression lists at the API layer; reject or quarantine addresses that generate hard bounces, and feed complaint data back into the tenant’s dashboard for immediate corrective action.  
- Deploy feedback‑loop integration with major ISPs (Google, Microsoft, Yahoo) and automate reputation scoring; throttle or pause sending for any tenant whose spam complaint rate exceeds 0.1 % over a 24‑hour window.  

Example: When a SaaS provider migrated 12 SMB tenants to tenant‑specific subdomains and introduced per‑tenant DKIM keys, their overall inbox placement rose from 78 % to 93 % within two weeks, and the average bounce rate dropped from 2.4 % to 0.6 %, significantly.
