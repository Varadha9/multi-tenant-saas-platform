## Research Note

Spring Boot’s native support for Hibernate filters enables lightweight, schema‑shared multi‑tenancy with minimal performance overhead for SMB SaaS platforms.  
- Configure a single datasource and enable Hibernate’s @FilterDef and @Filter annotations on each entity, injecting the tenant identifier at request time via a OncePerRequestFilter that sets a ThreadLocal context.
