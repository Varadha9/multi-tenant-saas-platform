## Research Note

Key insight: For SMB‑focused multi‑tenant SaaS, product‑led growth accelerates adoption, but a hybrid that layers lightweight sales touchpoints on high‑value accounts yields the fastest ARR lift.  

- Build a self‑service onboarding flow that provisions isolated tenants in under 5 minutes, leveraging infrastructure‑as‑code (Terraform) and container orchestration (Kubernetes) to auto‑scale per‑tenant resources without manual ops.  
- Embed usage‑based telemetry (OpenTelemetry) into core services to surface real‑time health and feature adoption dashboards, enabling data‑driven product nudges and reducing churn by surfacing friction early.  
- Design a modular billing engine with pluggable pricing tiers (per‑seat, usage, feature add‑ons) that can be toggled via API, allowing sales reps to customize contracts without code changes.  
- Implement a shared‑database sharding strategy (row‑level tenant isolation) combined with row‑level security policies, balancing security compliance for SMB data with the low latency needed for a frictionless trial experience.
