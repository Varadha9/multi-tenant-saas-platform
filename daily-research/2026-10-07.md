## Research Note

Centralized, immutable audit logs with tenant isolation are the linchpin for SaaS compliance and trust.  
- Store logs in append‑only, tamper‑evident storage (e.g., CloudWatch Logs with KMS‑encrypted S3 archive) and enforce a retention policy of ≥ 7 years to satisfy GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO 27001.  
- Tag each log entry with a tenant identifier and a cryptographic hash chain per tenant, enabling per‑tenant forensic queries without exposing other customers' data.  
- Emit logs asynchronously via a reliable message bus (Kafka or AWS EventBridge) and batch them for near‑real‑time delivery to a SIEM, ensuring < 2 seconds latency for critical events like admin credential changes.  
- Implement role‑based access control for log retrieval, limiting read access to tenant‑specific logs for support staff while granting auditors read‑only access to the immutable archive via fine‑grained IAM policies.
