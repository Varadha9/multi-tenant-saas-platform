## Research Note

High churn in SMB multi‑tenant SaaS is driven more by product‑fit and operational friction than by price alone.  
When SMBs evaluate a multi‑tenant platform, friction points in performance, onboarding, and ongoing value perception surface quickly, making churn a leading KPI for product health.  

- Implement usage telemetry per tenant to detect early drop‑off patterns (e.g., >30% decline in daily active users over 7 days) and trigger automated re‑engagement workflows and feed the data into a real‑time dashboard for ops teams.  
- Enforce strict tenant isolation with schema‑per‑tenant or row‑level security, then layer a shared‑service health dashboard to surface latency spikes that correlate with churn spikes.  
- Deploy a modular onboarding pipeline that auto‑scales feature flags based on SMB maturity tier, reducing feature overload and shortening time‑to‑value to <48 hours.  
- Integrate a predictive churn model into the billing microservice, scoring each tenant nightly and feeding alerts into CRM for targeted success‑manager outreach.  

A mid‑market SaaS provider that added per‑tenant usage alerts saw its 30‑day churn drop from 5.8% to 3.2% within six months, a 45% reduction, after correlating a 2‑week latency increase of >150 ms with churn spikes.
